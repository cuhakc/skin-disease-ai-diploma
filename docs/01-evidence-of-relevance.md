## Relevance summary and limitations

The survey provides exploratory evidence of skin concerns, information-seeking, prior app use and willingness to consider a preliminary assessment, while dermatologist visits were less common. Respondents identified accuracy, speed, privacy, free use, understandable explanations, visible confidence and referral advice as relevant considerations. These findings inform the existing decision-support, privacy and usability requirements and the proposed comparison of server and on-device processing. They do not establish clinical performance or population-wide demand.

Limitations: n=20; non-probability convenience sample; 16 of 20 respondents were aged 18–20 and 13 were students; only 3 had medical education or work experience; self-reported intentions are not behavior; there was no specialist validation of need; the sample is not representative of the population of Kazakhstan.

Unvalidated assumptions:
- The app improves access to dermatologists.
- Photo-based preliminary assessment is accurate enough for users.
- Users in other age groups share these preferences.
